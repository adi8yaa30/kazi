The Kazi Collective

Vikram Aditya Saikia

Founder | Creative Director

Creative Direction Is Where Strategy Finds Its Voice. At KAZI, It Shapes Everything From Brand Identity And Storytelling To Campaigns, Digital Experiences And Creative Systems That Help Businesses Grow With Purpose.

Drawing From A Multidisciplinary Background In Writing, Film And Design, Vikram Leads The Studio's Creative Vision, Bringing Together People, Ideas And Execution Into Work That Is Thoughtful, Commercially Driven And Unmistakably Human.

Ankita Saikia

Co-Founder | Marketing Director

Marketing At KAZI Moves Well Beyond The Screen. The Department Brings Together Campaign Strategy, Performance Marketing, Client Servicing And Graphic Communication Across Digital And Physical Spaces—From Social Media And Campaign Creatives To Signages, Hoardings, Vehicle Wraps, Promotional Collateral, Outdoor Advertising And Other Brand-Facing Externals.

Ankita Leads This Space With Over A Decade Of Marketing Experience And A Strong Understanding Of How Brands Should Present Themselves Across Different Environments. Her Strength Lies In Taking Projects From Strategy To Rollout—Coordinating People, Media And Deliverables While Keeping The Brand Consistent Wherever It Appears. That Ability To Carry Ideas Through Execution Has Become An Important Part Of KAZI's Long-Standing Client Relationships

Aratrika Tarafdar

Communications & Operations

Operations Are The Foundation That Allows Creativity To Thrive. From Project Planning And Internal Coordination To Client Communication And Execution, This Department Ensures Every Moving Part Works Together Seamlessly. Aratrika Leads These Efforts With Clarity, Organisation And A People-First Approach, Providing The Structure That Keeps KAZI's Creative Momentum Moving Forward.

Amrit Xess

D.O.P

Production Is Where Ideas Become Reality. Every Frame, Every Composition And Every Moment Captured Contributes To Telling A Brand's Story With Authenticity And Precision. Amrit Leads KAZI's Production Efforts Across Commercial Campaigns, Documentaries And Live Productions, Bringing A Calm Presence, Technical Discipline And An Intuitive Eye For Cinematic Storytelling To Every Project.
